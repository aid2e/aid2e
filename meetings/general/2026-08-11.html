Monitoring Goals

  • Use AID2E and PanDA logs and outputs to monitor optimization jobs.
  • Identify why jobs fail and at which stage.
  • If a failure is likely to affect other jobs, suggest stopping queued jobs.
  • Use previous optimization results to suggest new design points.
  • Allow offline searches of past optimization jobs, including failures, results, and performance information.
  • Add PanDA support for accessing locally stored optimization results.
  • Temporarily store PanDA logs centrally for access to ongoing and recent jobs.
